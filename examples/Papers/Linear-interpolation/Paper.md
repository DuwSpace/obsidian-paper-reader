# Linear interpolation: a reading demo

This original example is a mathematical note, not a summary of a research paper.

## Interpolation path

For two vectors $x_0$ and $x_1$, define a path with parameter $t$:

$$
z_t=(1-t)x_0+tx_1, \qquad 0\le t\le 1.
$$

![Linear interpolation, increasing from the lower left to the upper right](attachments/linear-path.png)

The derivative is $\frac{d z_t}{d t}=x_1-x_0$. Selecting this paragraph together with the equation and image supplies all three kinds of context to the reader.
